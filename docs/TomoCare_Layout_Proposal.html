The key choice:

At compact widths, care details become a temporary overlay. On phones, a smaller Tomo sits above the conversation. The open transcript never covers the speaking controls.

Keep context

Preserve the open transcript, selected chart units, and entered text when panels move or modes change.

Keep control

Use visible labels, reachable actions, and an accessible close path. Avoid translucent care details over Tomo’s face.

Keep it bounded

Use available height for scrollable content. Dialog headers and dismissal actions remain reachable on short windows.

Proportional design sketches, not screenshots or an implemented app. Voice, chart, and navigation content inside the sketches is illustrative. Date wording and exact breakpoints remain proposals. On small viewing windows, the desktop sketch reflows for legibility.
