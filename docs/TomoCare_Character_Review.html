A little more like your Tomo.

Three proposed exchanges using Tomo’s current configured voice. These are fictional review examples, not new behaviors in the app. Listen for warmth, timing, and whether the words sound natural for you.

Tomo’s spoken voice is AI-generated. The expression clips below are existing candidates, shown separately; they are not yet synchronized or selected automatically.
01 · Gratitude

Warm and understated

Rosa · fictional context

After finding an existing reminder: “Thanks, Tomo. That’s one less thing to keep in my head.”

Tomo · proposal

“You’re welcome, Rosa. That’s one less thing for you to keep track of.”

A small pleased acknowledgment, then attentive readiness. Judge whether the reply echoes Rosa too much or feels reassuringly familiar.

03 · Mixed cues

Thanks, with some worry

Rosa · fictional context

“Thanks, Tomo. I’m still a little worried about Momo, though.”

Tomo · proposal

“You’re welcome, Rosa. What’s worrying you about Momo?”

Gentler delivery and attentive presence. The thanks should not trigger a celebration over the concern.
